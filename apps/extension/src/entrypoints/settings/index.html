Privacy

When you choose Sign In or Save, L@tr.link sends your account authorization, the selected page URL, and your tags to L@tr.link to save the item and build its preview.

Page previews read metadata from the selected tab. The extension does not collect browsing history in the background.

Tags

Press Enter or comma to add a tag. Tags can contain spaces, and matching is exact and case-sensitive.
